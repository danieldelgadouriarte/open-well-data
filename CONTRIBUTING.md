# Contributing to Open Well Data™

Thank you for helping. Issues with corrections, new sources and benchmark results are welcome.

It is licensed PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 (code) and CC BY-NC 4.0 (catalogue, dictionary, schema and standardized data), with separate commercial licences (see [NOTICE](NOTICE)). To keep one owner able to license the whole project, **every contribution is accepted only under the agreement below**. Without it, a contribution cannot be merged.

How to agree: in your first pull request or submission, add this sentence to the description, with your name and the date:

> I have read the Open Well Data Contributor Licence Agreement, version 1.0, and I agree to it for this and all my future contributions. — *Full name, email, date*

Also add `Signed-off-by: Your Name <email>` to each commit. If you contribute on behalf of your employer, the employer must agree too (email contact@openwelldata.org before contributing).

## What to send

- A new source: its landing page, who publishes it, the licence as written, and how the data is accessed.
- A correction: the record id, what is wrong and the evidence (a link or a quote).
- A benchmark result: your code and the test metrics on the fixed split.

Questions: contact@openwelldata.org.
